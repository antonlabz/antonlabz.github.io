Privacy policy

"rclone pi" is a personal tool used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own files from a home server to the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

  • Who can use it: only the owner. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • What it accesses: the owner's own Google Drive, only to upload, list and remove the owner's backup files.
  • What is collected: nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive itself.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • Storage: the Google sign-in token stays on the owner's home server; backups are encrypted before upload.
